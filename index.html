        How does ClauseClear spot unfair terms in a contract? ▼

        ClauseClear runs real-time heuristic natural language pattern recognition against established legal standards. It checks for binding arbitration clauses, auto-renewals, non-refundable policies, broad intellectual property assignments, and unilateral modification rights without notice.

        Is my contract data safe and private when using this tool? ▼

        Yes, completely. Unlike tools that send your documents to third-party cloud servers or large language model APIs, ClauseClear performs 100% of its analysis right inside your browser window. No text, confidential agreements, or names ever leave your device.

        What should I do if a contract has red flags? ▼

        If red flags are found (such as an auto-renewal with strict cancellation periods or arbitration waivers), you can: (1) Set calendar reminders before the cancellation window closes, (2) Check if the company provides an arbitration opt-out letter within the first 30 days, or (3) Request the counterparty to strike out specific predatory clauses before signing.
