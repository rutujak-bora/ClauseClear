        Subscription & Auto-Renewal Traps

        Many services enroll users in recurring billing that is difficult to cancel. We scan for cancellation time limits (like required 60-day notices) and non-refundable clauses.

        ⚖️

        Arbitration & Jury Waivers

        Standard boilerplate agreements often force users into private binding arbitration, waiving your constitutional right to take complaints before a public court or class action.

        🔒

        Data Monetization & Privacy

        We flag provisions that permit companies to sell user information to data brokers or advertising networks, while noting whether you retain GDPR/CCPA data deletion rights.
