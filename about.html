About ClauseClear

ClauseClear was created to solve a universal consumer headache: nobody reads the terms of service, yet everyone is bound by them.

Our Mission

Standard terms of service and software end-user licensing agreements average between 5,000 and 15,000 words—taking upwards of 45 minutes to read. Hidden within those walls of text are recurring billing cycles, non-refundable clauses, arbitration waivers, and third-party data tracking.

Our mission is to level the playing field by providing everyday internet users with an instant, private, free scanner that extracts red flags in seconds.

How ClauseClear Works

ClauseClear employs structured natural language heuristics and legal clause pattern matchers that evaluate contracts across three dimensions:

  • Red Flags: Automatic renewal traps, mandatory arbitration waivers, early termination penalties, and unilateral modification rights.
  • Caution Items: Broad intellectual property licenses, third-party data broker sharing, and liability disclaimers.
  • Consumer Protections: Explicit data deletion provisions, refund windows, and reasonable notice periods.

Privacy By Design

Unlike tools that send your documents to third-party databases, ClauseClear evaluates your text directly inside your local browser instance. Your sensitive documents, leases, and contracts stay on your device.
